FAQ

Common questions before taking the next step.

Find answers about getting started and organizing your business with Entreprenly.

Do I need to know technology to use Entreprenly? +

No. The product is designed for merchants who need a simple, clear tool they can apply from day one.

Can I start without integrating everything from the beginning? +

Yes. Start with the essentials and add features when your operation needs them.

What is the difference between monthly and annual billing? +

The monthly plan costs S/ 89 per month. The annual option costs S/ 79 per month, billed month to month with a 12-month commitment.

Does it help if I already sell through WhatsApp every day? +

That is exactly where much of the value is: organizing that channel, reducing errors, and connecting it better with daily operations.
